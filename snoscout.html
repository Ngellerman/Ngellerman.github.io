SnoScout

A ground-based search-and-rescue rover that autonomously localizes buried avalanche beacons — pairing a software-defined radio with GPS-encoder sensor fusion to pinpoint a victim before the rescue window closes.

Role  Hardware Integration · Signals Stack  ESP 32 · Serial Communications · GNU Radio Year  2025

Demo — Technical overview and system explanation of SnoScout including driving and beacon detection demonstration. Watch on YouTube ↗

How it works

SnoScout listens for the standard 457 kHz avalanche-transceiver pulse using a software-defined radio feeding a custom GNU Radio pipeline that extracts and tracks the signal in real time. As the rover drives, a GPS-encoder sensor fusion system tags every signal-strength reading with a precise position.

Those SNR-weighted geospatial samples are clustered with DBSCAN to reject noise and converge on the most probable beacon location — turning a scatter of noisy radio readings into a single coordinate a rescuer can dig at.
