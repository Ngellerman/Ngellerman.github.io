Particle Accelerator Sensor Array

Designed a vacuum-based alignment jig for the Silicon Vertex Detector on the Electron Ion Collider, hitting 0.14mm precision across ~2,300 sensor mountings. Delivered 20 silicon PCB arrays validated at CERN and now in active use at LBNL.

NASA MicroG Next - Passive Capture Tool Dock

A passive capture tool dock for spacewalk operations in low Earth orbit, selected for NASA's Micro-g NExT program. The dual-actuation, single-fault-tolerant locking mechanism is machined from 6061-T6 aluminum and clears all 16 NASA compliance requirements — holding a 15 lb retention load at a 2.0+ safety factor while weighing just 0.69 lb inside a 2.5 × 3.97 × 2.99 in envelope.

Robot Lego Assembler

A full-stack robotic pipeline, built in ROS2, that autonomously assembles arbitrary Duplo Lego builds with a UR7e arm. A 3D point-cloud vision system pairs DBSCAN clustering with LAB color classification to read brick poses and colors at a near-100% detection rate, while an inverse-kinematics motion planner lands placements at 85% success under tight press-fit tolerances.

SnoScout - Advanced Avalanche Rescue

A ground-based search-and-rescue rover that autonomously homes in on buried avalanche beacons. A software-defined radio feeds a custom GNU Radio pipeline that extracts the 457kHz beacon signal in real time, and a GPS-encoder sensor fusion system uses DBSCAN clustering over SNR-weighted geospatial data to pinpoint where the victim lies.
